Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Welcome to Omnipost. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the data and personal information you provide when using our multi-platform video publishing software.

1. Overview

Omnipost is a self-hosted, personal content distribution tool designed to help creators upload videos simultaneously to YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and X (Twitter). Omnipost operates primarily locally on your own machine.

2. Information We Collect and Process

When you use Omnipost, the application processes the following types of information:

  • Account Credentials & OAuth Tokens: Client IDs, Client Secrets, access tokens, and refresh tokens generated via official OAuth flows (Google/YouTube, Meta/Facebook/Instagram, TikTok, and X/Twitter).
  • Profile Information: Basic public profile details (such as your account username, display name, channel ID, and avatar) fetched through official platform APIs solely to display your connected status in the user interface.
  • Content Data: Video files, titles, descriptions, captions, and tags that you explicitly choose to upload to social networks.

3. How Your Data Is Stored and Used

  • Local Storage: All API keys, OAuth tokens, and video files remain on your local computer. We do not operate external third-party database servers to harvest or aggregate your credentials.
  • Direct API Communication: All requests to publish content or retrieve profile information are sent directly between your local instance and the respective official social media APIs.
  • No Selling or Sharing of Data: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade any user data or credentials with third parties.

4. Third-Party Platforms and APIs

Omnipost connects to the following third-party developer APIs. Your use of these platforms through Omnipost is also subject to their respective privacy policies:

  • Google / YouTube Privacy Policy and YouTube Terms of Service
  • Meta (Facebook & Instagram) Privacy Policy
  • TikTok Privacy Policy
  • X (Twitter) Privacy Policy

5. Data Retention and Revocation

You may disconnect any connected social media account or delete your stored configuration at any time directly through the Omnipost settings panel or by deleting the local database file (data/store.json). You can also revoke Omnipost's access permissions at any time directly in your Google, Meta, TikTok, or X account security settings.

6. User Data Deletion Instructions

For instructions on requesting data deletion or removing permissions for your accounts, please visit our User Data Deletion Instructions page.
