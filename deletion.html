User Data Deletion Instructions

Last Updated: October 2026

Omnipost respects your privacy and control over your personal data. In accordance with platform developer policies (including Meta Platform Data Deletion Requirements and GDPR/CCPA regulations), this page explains how you can remove your data and revoke application permissions.

How to Delete Your Data from Omnipost

Because Omnipost operates as a self-hosted client on your own computer, all data is stored locally on your machine. You can remove all data immediately through any of the following methods:

Option 1: Disconnect Within the App

  1. Open your Omnipost dashboard (e.g. http://localhost:3000).
  2. Navigate to the Settings / Accounts tab.
  3. Click Disconnect next to Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, or X.
  4. This immediately deletes the stored access tokens and profile information from your local configuration.

Option 2: Clear Local Data Storage

You can completely erase all credentials and history by deleting the local data store file on your computer:

omnipost/data/store.json

Option 3: Revoke Permissions on Connected Platforms

You can revoke Omnipost's access directly on each platform at any time:

  • Facebook & Instagram: Go to Facebook Settings & Privacy > Settings > Apps and Websites, find Omnipost, and click Remove.
  • Google / YouTube: Go to your Google Account Permissions and remove access for Omnipost.
  • TikTok: Go to TikTok app Settings > Security & Permissions > Manage App Permissions and remove Omnipost.
  • X (Twitter): Go to X Settings & Privacy > Security and account access > Apps and sessions > Connected apps and revoke access.
